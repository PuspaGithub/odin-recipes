MOMO

Description

A momo is a type of bite-sized, filled dumpling that originated in the Himalayas (primarily Tibet and Nepal), featuring a thin wheat-flour dough wrapper folded by hand into a purse or crescent shape.

Ingredients

  • Dough Wrapper
  • Traditional Meat Filling
  • Vegetarian Filling
  • The Essentail Dipping Sauce

Recipe

  1. Mix flour and Water
  2. Let it rest
  3. season the grounded chicken
  4. Add 1tbsp of vegetable oil or melted ghee
  5. Roll the dough into small pieces
  6. Flatten the dough, put the filling inside it and wrap
  7. Steam the momo
